Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Quizzigo. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data within our quiz application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identification information unless voluntarily provided. Our application primarily uses browser local storage to save your game progress, coins, and settings locally on your device.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ad Settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our application to improve the user experience. This data is aggregated and does not identify individuals.

Data Security

Since most of your data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We implement industry-standard practices for any data processed via our servers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our main website.
